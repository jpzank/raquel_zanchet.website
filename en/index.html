How the work unfolds

01

Hospitality Design

Concept, positioning, interior design and guest experience. It settles what the hotel is, before any decision on site.

02

FF&E Curation

Turns the concept into a buyable list, area by area, with selection, specification and supplier recommendations.

03

Hotel Implementation

Prototype room, OS&E, installation, styling and pre-opening. The stage where the project becomes a working hotel.
